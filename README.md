# GOGYO — Find your flow

A culturally inspired city discovery experience connecting the five elements (五行), real places, and small actions for your day.

Enter your birthday to discover your day-stem element, choose a destination by text or voice, and receive an activity inspired by your element and today’s element. Available in English and Japanese.

- **Five elements:** a transparent, date-based interpretation with an animated Three.js wheel.
- **Live discovery:** Tavily searches for place information and a self-hosted Ollama model generates an activity with a source link.
- **Explore:** view the suggested location on Google Maps and switch languages.

**Live demo:** [GOGYO](https://gogyo-flow.jazzybell512.chatgpt.site/)

See [fiveflow/README.md](fiveflow/README.md) for setup, environment variables, and limitations. This is a cultural experience, not a scientific prediction.
